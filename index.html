    Meet the author

    Hi, I’m Abby Knox.

    I write upbeat romance with plenty of heat. Readers call my books quirky, sexy, adorable and funny — and I’ll take all four.

    Fantasy Abby

    Dreams of a farm with Ryan Gosling, goats and chickens — or a cottage in a magical wood as a historical re-enactor.

    Reality Abby

    Has zero interest in mucking out stalls or wearing bloomers. So she writes the daydreams into romance novels instead.

    Raised on Buffy, Gilmore Girls and LOST — but I promise you’ll never suffer through a Luke-and-Lorelai-sized rough patch in my books.
